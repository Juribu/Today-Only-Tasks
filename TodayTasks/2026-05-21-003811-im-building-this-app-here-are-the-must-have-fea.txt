❯ what does .allowsHitTesting(false) do                                         

⏺ .allowsHitTesting(false) makes the view (and its subviews) ignore touch input
  — taps pass straight through to whatever is behind it.

  In CelebrationView, it's on the outer ZStack (which includes the dimmed
  background and the card). Without it, the full-screen Color.black.opacity(...)
   would intercept taps for the ~1.15s the celebration is visible, blocking the
  user from tapping other tasks. With it on, the celebration is purely visual
  and the list underneath stays interactive.
